Tricks worth knowing

  • ×4 — double, then double again.
  • ×8 — double three times.
  • ×9 — ×10 then subtract one lot. The digits always sum to 9.
  • ×11 (to 9) — repeat the digit: 7 × 11 = 77.
  • ×12 — ×10 plus ×2.
  • ×5 — half of ×10.
